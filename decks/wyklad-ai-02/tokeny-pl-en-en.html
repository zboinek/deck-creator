Token

A token is a piece of text, not a word

The model gets a vocabulary of pieces, mostly ones common in English. Polish words have to be assembled from them.
On a longer sentence the gap is smaller: for the fly on the coat rack sentence GPT-4o uses 27 tokens in Polish and 19 in English. Polish costs more, but newer vocabularies cut it more and more sensibly.
Own measurement: tiktoken, encodings gpt2, cl100k_base and o200k_base. A year later: Bielik v3 moved to its own Polish vocabulary.
1500 tokens ≈ 1 A4 page
