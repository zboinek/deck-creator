Béchamel is a white sauce of butter, flour and milk [1]. For one portion of pasta:
  1. 1 tbsp butter, hob on medium.
  2. Add 1 tbsp flour and stir for a minute. This is a roux: it should smell like a biscuit, not turn brown.
  3. Pour in a glass of milk bit by bit, stirring with a fork. Lumps? Stir harder, they'll go.
  4. Done when it coats a spoon. Salt, pepper.
