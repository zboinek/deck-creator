Step 2: chunks

The same recipe, two ways to cut it

A chunk has to make sense on its own, because it goes into the prompt without the rest of the document.

By length

fixed length (about 100 characters) and overlap
#1Zucchini fritters. Ingredients: 2 zucchini, 2 eggs, 4 tbsp flour, salt, pepper, oil for fr✂
#2pepper, oil for frying. Method: Grate the zucchini and squeeze out the water. Add the eggs✂
#3water. Add the eggs and flour, season. Fry in hot oil for 3 minutes on each side.
Simple and works for any text. But #3 does not know it is about fritters: it may not come up for a question about fritters.

By structure

by document sections, the title attached to each
#1Zucchini fritters › Ingredients2 zucchini, 2 eggs, 4 tbsp flour, salt, pepper, oil for frying.
#2Zucchini fritters › MethodGrate the zucchini and squeeze out the water. Add the eggs and flour, season. Fry in hot oil for 3 minutes on each side.
Every piece knows what it is about. It needs structure, meaning headings: easy in DOCX and HTML, hard in PDF. Sections that are too long still get cut further by length.
Highlight: the overlap, text repeated in two chunks so that a sentence on the boundary is not lost.
chunk = meaning + context
