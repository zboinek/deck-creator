Step 3: resultant vector

A text's vector is its word arrows placed tip to tail

The arrow from the start of the first to the end of the last is the resultant vector: one vector for the whole sentence.

Word2vec: just the sum

every word has a fixed vector, whatever the sentence
“bat” is always an animal. Word order does not matter either: “dog bites man” gives the same sum as “man bites dog”.

Transformer embedder: attention, then the sum

words first refine each other, as in L9
Next to “glove” and “helmet”, the vector of “bat” turns towards sports. Same words, a different resultant vector.
“Have you seen my bat? I left it with my baseball glove and helmet.” 2 dimensions, illustrative numbers. The average is the same arrow, only shorter.
resultant ≠ sum of words
