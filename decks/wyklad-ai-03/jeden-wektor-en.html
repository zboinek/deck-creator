A question about one detail from chapter 7 is compared with a vector that “talks” about the whole book. The detail gets lost in the average. That is why we cut documents into chunks and each chunk gets its own vector. Besides, the whole book would not fit anyway: ada-002 accepts at most 8191 tokens.
