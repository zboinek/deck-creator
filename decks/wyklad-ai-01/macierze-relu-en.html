Matrices + activations

Why AI is more than matrix multiplication

An activation function is simple. What is complex is the function you get by interleaving it with matrices.
01 · no activation

Matrices only

y = W₃·W₂·W₁·x
  = W·x
1000 layers = 1 layer
The matrices can be multiplied in advance. Depth disappears.
vs
02 · with activation

Matrices + ReLU

y = W₃·ReLU(W₂·ReLU(W₁·x))
Cannot be collapsed
Which neurons let the signal through depends on the input.
→
03 · result

A tent from three ReLUs

f(x) = ReLU(x)
− 2·ReLU(x−1) + ReLU(x−2)
A shape no matrix can make
A piecewise linear function. It bends wherever another ReLU switches on.
ReLU(x) = max(0, x). LLMs add attention, softmax and normalisation on top.
Simple switch × billions = complex function
