Where does “bat” belong?

The vector of “bat” gets pulled towards whatever the rest of the sentence is about.
Sentence A
A bat is a mammal. It sleeps hanging upside down.
→ moves towards animals
Sentence B
Have you seen my bat? I left it with my baseball glove and helmet.
→ moves towards sports
The attention mechanism lets the model understand meaning depending on the context.
